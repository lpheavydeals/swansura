## Returns & Refunds

We want you to love everything you order from Swansura – The Body Studio. If something isn't right, here's how returns and refunds work. This policy doesn't affect your legal rights.

### 30-day returns on unopened items

You can return any item within **30 days of receiving it** for a full refund, as long as it is:

- **unopened and unused**, with any seal, cellophane wrap or tamper sticker still intact
- in its **original packaging**, in a condition we can resell

This covers everything we sell, including sale items, gift sets and advent calendars, as long as they're unopened.

### Why we can't accept opened items

For hygiene and safety reasons, we can't take back skincare, haircare, bath and body products or fragrances once they have been opened, unsealed or used, unless they are faulty (see below). This is allowed under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 for sealed goods that are unsuitable for return once unsealed. It protects every customer, because nothing we sell has been opened or used before.

### Faulty, damaged, leaking or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged or leaking, or it's faulty or not what you ordered, please email us at info@swansura.com within [48 hours] of delivery. Include your order number and a photo of the item and the packaging.

We'll arrange a replacement or a full refund, **including the original delivery charge**. You won't pay to send anything back. Often we won't need the item returned at all.

If a product turns out to be faulty after you've started using it, contact us within 30 days of delivery. Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, you're entitled to a refund, repair or replacement. If you have a reaction to a product, stop using it and contact us; we'll help you and pass the batch details to the brand.

### Your right to cancel

Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, you can cancel your order **within 14 days of delivery** without giving a reason. Opened items are subject to the hygiene exception above.

To cancel, email info@swansura.com with your order number. Then send the items back within 14 days of telling us.

Our 30-day returns window above is in addition to these rights.

### How to return an item

1. **Email us** at info@swansura.com with your order number and the items you'd like to return. Please do this before sending anything, because we can't accept returns we haven't been told about.
2. **We'll reply within [1 working day]** with your return details [and a prepaid return label].
3. **Pack the items securely** in their original packaging, and include your order number.
4. **Send them to:** Swansura – The Body Studio, [14 Bendish Road, East Ham, London E6 1JH], United Kingdom.

**Return postage:** [Option A, recommended: returns are free; we'll email you a prepaid label.] [Option B: unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong item, return postage is paid by you. We recommend a tracked service, because we can't refund items that don't reach us.]

### Refunds

Once your return arrives, we'll inspect it and email you to confirm. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within **[10] working days**, and always within 14 days of us receiving the item, as the law requires.

If you return a whole order within the 14-day cancellation period, we'll also refund the original standard delivery charge. If you chose Express, we refund only the cost of standard delivery.

Your bank or card provider may take a few extra days to show the refund. If it hasn't appeared 10 working days after our approval email, please contact us.

Orders paid with Klarna or Clearpay [confirm providers] are refunded to that account, and your instalment plan updates automatically.

### Exchanges

The quickest way to get a different item is to return the unopened item for a refund and place a new order.

### Discount codes, free gifts and bundles

If you return part of an order that used a discount code or a bundle price, such as a Duo or Routine pack, we'll refund the amount you actually paid for that item. Free gifts don't need to be returned unless you're returning the whole order.

### Gift cards

Gift cards can't be refunded or exchanged for cash.

### Items bought as gifts

Gift recipients can return unopened items within 30 days of delivery for a gift card or store credit. The original buyer can choose a refund to the original payment method instead.

Swansura – The Body Studio, [business address], London, United Kingdom

## What changed from the current policy, and why (not part of the policy)
