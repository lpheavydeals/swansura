## Shipping Policy

Swansura – The Body Studio ships every order from our studio in London. All prices are in pounds sterling (GBP) and include VAT.

### Where we deliver

We currently deliver to addresses in the **United Kingdom** only, including Northern Ireland, the Scottish Highlands and Islands, the Isle of Man and the Channel Islands [confirm the last two]. We are not able to ship outside the UK at the moment. Many of our fragrances count as flammable goods, which limits international shipping.

### Delivery options and costs

| Service | Order value | Cost | Delivery time after dispatch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard UK delivery | Under £[50] | £[2.00 up to £20 / £3.00 from £20] | [2–3] working days |
| Standard UK delivery | £[50] and over | **Free** | [2–3] working days |
| Express UK delivery | Any | £[4.00] | [1–2] working days |

The delivery cost is shown at checkout before you pay. Orders to the Scottish Highlands and Islands, Northern Ireland and offshore addresses can take [1–2] extra working days.

### When we dispatch

Orders placed before [1pm] UK time, Monday to Friday, are usually packed and dispatched [the same or the next] working day. Orders placed after that time, or at the weekend or on a UK bank holiday, are dispatched on the next working day. During sale periods and the run-up to Christmas, dispatch can take up to [2] extra working days. We will show this on the website when it applies.

### Tracking

When your order leaves us, we email you a dispatch confirmation with your tracking number and a link to follow your parcel with [Royal Mail / Evri / DPD]. If you have not received a dispatch email within [3] working days of ordering, please contact us.

### Christmas and seasonal deadlines

We publish our last order dates for Christmas delivery on the website each year. Orders placed after those dates will still be sent, but we cannot guarantee delivery before Christmas.

### If your parcel is late, lost or damaged

- **Late:** if your parcel has not arrived within [7] working days of dispatch, contact us at info@swansura.com with your order number. We will chase it with the courier.
- **Lost:** if the courier confirms the parcel is lost, we will send a replacement or give you a full refund, including the delivery charge.
- **Damaged, leaking or wrong item:** please check your order when it arrives. Email us within [48 hours] at info@swansura.com with your order number and a photo of the item and the packaging, and we will send a replacement or refund at no cost to you.

Your legal rights are not affected. Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, your order remains our responsibility until it is delivered to you or to someone you have nominated.

### Delivery address

Please make sure your address and phone number are correct at checkout. If a parcel is returned to us because the address was incomplete or wrong, we will contact you to arrange redelivery, and a new delivery charge may apply. If you need to change your address after ordering, email us as soon as possible. We can only change it before the order is dispatched.

### Missed deliveries

If nobody is in, the courier will leave a card or send a notification explaining how to rebook delivery or collect your parcel. Parcels not collected within the courier's holding period are returned to us. We will then contact you about redelivery.

### Returns

For returns and refunds, please see our [Refund Policy](/policies/refund-policy).

We aim to reply within [1 working day], Monday to Friday.
