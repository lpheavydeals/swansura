### Body

<p><strong>Oud</strong> is one of the most talked-about notes in perfume: deep, woody and a little smoky. Designer houses such as Tom Ford, Gucci, Guerlain and Chopard each have their own take on it. Here is what oud is, how the best-known <strong>oud perfumes</strong> differ, and how to choose and wear one.</p>
<h2>What is oud?</h2>
<p>Oud, also called agarwood or aoud, is a dark, fragrant resin that forms in the wood of agar (Aquilaria) trees. It has been treasured in the Middle East and South Asia for centuries, burned as incense and worn as an oil. Natural oud is rare, so many modern perfumes use an <em>oud accord</em>: a blend of woods, resins and leathery notes built to give the same rich effect. Gucci, for example, lists an oud accord in its base notes.</p>

Its key notes are oud wood, rosewood and cardamom over sandalwood, vetiver, tonka bean and amber, so the oud reads smooth and warm rather than smoky. It is unisex and easy to wear in the office or in the evening. See every <a href="/collections/tom-ford">Tom Ford</a> fragrance.</p>
<h2>Gucci Intense Oud</h2>

Black pepper and olibanum (frankincense) open it; amber woods, sandalwood and cedarwood sit at the heart; leather, patchouli, gurjun balsam and the oud accord form the base. Expect incense and leather more than sweetness. Shop all <a href="/collections/gucci">Gucci</a>.</p>
<h2>Tom Ford Oud Wood or Gucci Intense Oud?</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Choose Oud Wood</strong> if you want a soft, creamy wood with a touch of spice that suits everyday wear.</li>
<li><strong>Choose Intense Oud</strong> if you like incense, leather and a bolder, smokier trail for evenings and colder months.</li>
</ul>

<ul>
<li><strong>New to oud:</strong> Tom Ford Oud Wood, or a fresher Amber Oud edition.</li>

<h2>How to wear oud</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Start small:</strong> one or two sprays. Oud notes are rich, and a little goes a long way.</li>
<li><strong>Apply to moisturised skin</strong> on the wrists, neck or chest. Our guide to <a href="/blogs/journal/layer-oud-with-body-butter">layering oud with body butter</a> explains why.</li>
<li><strong>Let it settle:</strong> do not rub your wrists together. Oud perfumes often change a lot after the first half hour, so judge them later in the day.</li>
<li><strong>Test before you buy</strong> a full bottle if you can, or start with a smaller size.</li>
</ol>

<li><strong>Store bottles</strong> away from heat and sunlight, with the cap on.</li>

### FAQs

- **What does oud smell like?** Deep, woody and slightly smoky, often with a leathery or resinous edge. Many perfumes soften it with rose, spice, amber or vanilla, so oud perfumes can range from creamy to dark and incense-like.
- **What is the difference between Tom Ford Oud Wood and Gucci Intense Oud?** Tom Ford Oud Wood is smoother and creamier: oud wood, rosewood and cardamom over sandalwood, vetiver, tonka bean and amber. Gucci Intense Oud is darker, with black pepper, frankincense, leather and patchouli.
- **Is oud perfume for men or women?** Both. Oud is traditionally worn by everyone. Tom Ford Oud Wood, Gucci Intense Oud and Guerlain Oud Essentiel are unisex; Chopard Oud Malaki is made for men and Montale Aoud Damascus for women.
- **Is the oud in perfume real?** Natural oud is rare and expensive, so many perfumes use an oud accord: a blend of woods, resins and leathery notes that recreates the effect. Gucci Intense Oud, for example, lists an oud accord in its base.

### Body

<p>The skin around your eyes is thinner than the rest of your face, and it moves every time you blink, smile or squint. That is why many people give it its own product. An <strong>under eye cream</strong> is a small, gentle moisturiser made for this delicate area. Here is how to choose one, how to apply it, and what to expect.</p>
<h2>Do you need an eye cream?</h2>
<p>You can use your face moisturiser around the eyes, as long as it does not sting. An eye cream is worth it if your moisturiser feels too rich or makes your eyes water, if the area feels dry or tight, or if you simply enjoy a separate step. Eye creams come in small jars, have a texture made for patting on, and are usually made without added fragrance.</p>
<h2>Cream, gel or balm?</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Light creams</strong> suit most skin and sit well under make-up.</li>
<li><strong>Gels</strong> feel cool and weightless, and suit oily skin or a morning routine.</li>
<li><strong>Balms and oils</strong> are richer, and suit dry skin or a night routine.</li>
</ul>

<h2>What about dark circles?</h2>
<p>Dark circles have many causes, from the shape of the face and the shadows it casts to genes, tiredness and allergies. An eye cream keeps the skin moisturised and comfortable, and a cool, light texture can make tired-looking eyes feel fresher, but no cream can promise to remove dark circles. Sleep, sunscreen and a little concealer often help more. If the area suddenly darkens, swells or itches, ask a pharmacist or your GP.</p>
<h2>How to apply eye cream</h2>
<ol>

### FAQs

- **Do I need an under eye cream?** Not always. You can use your face moisturiser around the eyes if it does not sting. An eye cream helps if your moisturiser feels too rich there, or if the area feels dry or tight.
- **Can an eye cream get rid of dark circles?** No cream can promise that. Dark circles have many causes, including face shape, genes and tiredness. An eye cream keeps the skin moisturised and comfortable; sleep, sunscreen and concealer often help more.
