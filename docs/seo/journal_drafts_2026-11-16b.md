## Deyga Beetroot Lip Balm: stick or pot, what is in it, and is it worth it?

/blogs/journal/deyga-beetroot-lip-balm-stick-or-pot · SEO title: Deyga Beetroot Lip Balm: Stick or Pot, Is It Worth It? · Description: Deyga Beetroot Lip Balm stick vs pot: the same seven ingredients, the price per gram, what it can and cannot do, and which one to buy in the UK.

**Quick answer:** the **Deyga Beetroot Lip Balm** comes as a 10g pot (£10.99) and a 5.5g twist-up stick (£13.99), and both have exactly the same seven ingredients. The pot works out at about £1.10 per gram and the stick at about £2.54 per gram, so the pot is the better value and the stick is the tidier one to carry. It is a moisturising balm with a sheer beetroot tint. It has no SPF, and no lip balm can promise to lighten your lips.

### What is in Deyga Beetroot Lip Balm?

The full ingredient list, in pack order, is the same for the [Classic pot](/products/deygas-beetroot-lip-balm-classic-10-g-100-natural-no-harsh-chemicals) and the [stick](/products/beetroot-lip-balm-stick-natural-tinted-balm-for-dry-lips-100-organic-ingredients-plant-based-cruelty-free-5-5-g):

- **Beetroot extract** (listed as Beta vulgaris, with its Sanskrit name, paldka): the only colour in the list, which gives the sheer pink tint.
- **Almond oil:** a light oil that helps the balm glide.
- **Avocado oil:** a richer oil for a cushioned feel.
- **Jojoba oil:** a light liquid wax.
- **Shea butter:** a soft plant butter.
- **Refined beeswax:** sets the balm and holds it on the lips.
- **Vitamin E.**

That is it: no water, no added fragrance and no preservative in the list, because a balm made only of oils, butter and wax does not need one. Because of the beeswax it is not vegan, and the almond oil matters if you have a nut allergy.

### Stick or pot: which should you buy?


- **Price:** pot £10.99 for 10g (about £1.10/g); stick £13.99 for 5.5g (about £2.54/g). Gram for gram the stick costs roughly 2.3 times as much.
- **Packs of two:** two pots are £20.99 (about £1.05/g) and two sticks £26.99 (about £2.45/g).
- **Applying:** the pot needs a fingertip; the stick twists up and goes straight on, which is easier on the move, in gloves or without a mirror.
- **Colour:** the same tint in both. With the stick, a second swipe builds it; with the pot, dab a little more.
- **Cold days:** the stick slips into a coat pocket; the pot is handy on a bedside table for a thicker layer at night.

Choose the **pot** for value and for night-time use. Choose the **stick** if you will actually carry it and reapply through the day.

### Does beetroot lip balm lighten dark lips?

You will see that claim online, including on some brand pages. We do not make it. Beetroot extract gives this balm a tint, so lips look pinker while it is on, and the oils and butter leave them feeling soft. That is a cosmetic effect that lasts while you wear it. Lips can darken for many reasons, from sun to smoking to medicines; if your lips change colour, or a patch is new or changing, show it to a pharmacist or your GP.

### Is the Deyga Beetroot Lip Balm worth it?

It is worth it if you want:

- a short, readable ingredient list with no added fragrance;
- a sheer, natural-looking tint rather than a lipstick colour;
- a balm that feels cushioned rather than waxy.

Look elsewhere if you need sun protection (it has no SPF), a strong colour, or a vegan balm. For daytime outdoors, pair it with [Vilvah Milk Lip Balm SPF 30 PA+++](/products/vilvah-milk-lip-balm-spf-30-pa-natural-uv-protection-hydrating-lip-care-for-all-skin-types) (£9.99). If you would rather skip the tint, [Deyga Shea Butter Lip Balm](/products/deyga-natural-vanilla-lip-balm-8g) (£9.99) is the plain everyday choice.

### How to use it


- Start with clean, dry lips.
- Once or twice a week, use a lip scrub first: [Deyga Mint Lip Scrub](/products/deyga-mint-lip-scrub-16g) (£12.99) or [Deyga Strawberry Lip Scrub](/products/strawberry-lip-scrub-16g-deygas-natural-exfoliating-lip-care-avocado-almond-oils-shea-butter-and-vitamin-e-hydrates-softens-smooths-lips) (£10.99), both sugar in avocado and almond oils with shea butter.
- Apply the balm in a thin layer; add a second for more tint.
- Reapply whenever lips feel dry, and add a thicker layer at night.

### What is in Vilvah Milk Drops?

The full ingredient list, in pack order:

- **Aqua** (water): the base of the gel.
- **Aloe vera juice:** a watery plant juice that gives a light, cool feel.
- **Betaine:** from sugar beet, it helps skin feel moisturised.
- **Glycerin:** a humectant that draws water into the skin's surface.
- **Xanthan gum:** thickens the serum into a gel.
- **Hyaluronic acid:** holds water, so skin feels plump and hydrated.
- **Caprylic/capric triglyceride:** a light, coconut-derived emollient.
- **Cystoseira tamariscifolia extract:** a brown seaweed extract.
- **Alpha arbutin:** a stable form of arbutin, a molecule found in plants such as bearberry.
- **Rice milk extract:** the milk in Milk Drops.
- **Sodium benzoate and potassium sorbate:** the preservatives.

Things the list does not contain: added fragrance, essential oils, alcohol, silicones or oils beyond the light triglyceride, and no animal-derived ingredients.

### Is Milk Drops a brightening serum?

Vilvah names it a brightening serum, and you will see that word on packs and online. We describe what is in it rather than promise a result: day to day it works as a lightweight, hydrating layer that sits well under moisturiser and makeup. We cannot promise any change to dark spots or skin tone. If a patch of pigmentation is new, changing or worrying you, show it to a pharmacist or your GP, and whatever serum you use, daily sunscreen is the step that matters most.
