### Body

<p><strong>Tom Ford perfume</strong> covers a wide range, from dark, velvety evening scents to bright citrus colognes. Here are the Tom Ford fragrances we stock, what is in each one, when to wear it, and a few other scents in a similar mood if you want to compare.</p>
<h2>Signature and Private Blend</h2>
<p>Tom Ford's perfumes fall into two main lines. The <strong>Signature</strong> collection includes Black Orchid and Noir Extreme. The <strong>Private Blend</strong> collection includes Oud Wood, Neroli Portofino and Ébène Fumé, and is usually sold in smaller bottles at a higher price. Both are made for anyone to wear: "for men" on a label is only a guide.</p>
<h2>Tom Ford Black Orchid</h2>
<p><a href="/products/tom-ford-black-orchid-parfum-100ml-unisex">Tom Ford Black Orchid Parfum</a> (£153.99, 100ml) is dark and rich. It opens on truffle and plum, moves into rum, ylang-ylang and black orchid, and settles on patchouli. It is an amber floral, made unisex, and best for evenings, special occasions and the colder months. The parfum is a stronger concentration than eau de parfum, so one or two sprays are plenty.</p>
<h2>Tom Ford Noir Extreme</h2>
<p><a href="/products/tom-ford-noir-extreme-parfum-100ml-for-men">Tom Ford Noir Extreme Parfum</a> (£144.99, 100ml) is warm and spicy-sweet. Cardamom, nutmeg, saffron, mandarin and neroli open it; rose, jasmine, orange blossom and kulfi, a milky, dessert-like accord, form the heart; vanilla, amber, sandalwood and woods make the base. It is made for men and suits evenings and cooler weather.</p>
<h2>Tom Ford Neroli Portofino</h2>
<p><a href="/products/tom-ford-neroli-portofino-eau-de-parfum-50ml-unisex">Tom Ford Neroli Portofino Eau de Parfum</a> (£164.99, 50ml) is the bright one. Italian bergamot, mandarin and Sicilian lemon lead into Tunisian neroli, orange flower and lavender, over a light base of amber and rosemary. It is unisex and easy to wear in the daytime, at work and on holiday.</p>
<h2>Ébène Fumé and Oud Wood</h2>
<p><a href="/products/tom-ford-ebene-fume-eau-de-parfum-50ml-unisex">Tom Ford Ébène Fumé</a> (£184.99, 50ml) is smoky and woody: palo santo and black pepper, then rose and leather, over ebony wood and resin. <a href="/products/tom-ford-oud-wood-eau-de-parfum-50ml-unisex">Tom Ford Oud Wood</a> (from £115.99, 30ml or 50ml) is smoother: oud wood, rosewood and cardamom over sandalwood, vetiver, tonka bean and amber. For more on oud, read our <a href="/blogs/journal/oud-perfume-guide">oud perfume guide</a>.</p>
<h2>Which Tom Ford perfume suits you?</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Fresh and easy for daytime:</strong> Neroli Portofino.</li>
<li><strong>Warm, spicy and sweet:</strong> Noir Extreme.</li>
<li><strong>Dark, rich and dramatic:</strong> Black Orchid.</li>
<li><strong>Smoky and woody:</strong> Ébène Fumé, or the softer Oud Wood.</li>
</ul>
<p>The season helps too. Neroli Portofino suits spring and summer, while Black Orchid, Noir Extreme, Ébène Fumé and Oud Wood come into their own in autumn and winter. Many people keep one fresh and one warm scent and switch as the weather changes.</p>
<h2>Other scents in a similar mood</h2>
<p>These are different perfumes, not copies, but they share some of the same notes:</p>
<ul>
<li>If you like Neroli Portofino's citrus: <a href="/products/acqua-di-parma-colonia-pura-eau-de-cologne-20ml-unisex">Acqua di Parma Colonia Pura</a> (£32.99, 20ml), with bergamot, orange and petitgrain over jasmine, coriander, musk and cedarwood.</li>
<li>If you like Noir Extreme's spice and vanilla: <a href="/products/joop-homme-le-parfum-75ml-men-s-warm-spicy-amber-fragrance">Joop! Homme Le Parfum</a> (£32.99, 75ml), with lavender and cinnamon, orange blossom and tonka bean, then vanilla, amber and woods.</li>
<li>If you like Black Orchid's rum and depth: <a href="/products/bvlgari-man-in-black-eau-de-parfum-100ml-luxury-men-s-fragrance">Bvlgari Man in Black</a> (£64.99, 100ml), with rum, spices and tobacco, leather, tuberose and iris, over guaiac wood, benzoin and tonka bean.</li>
</ul>
<h2>How to wear it</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Start with one or two sprays</strong> on the neck or wrists; parfums and eau de parfums are concentrated.</li>
<li><strong>Spray on moisturised skin</strong> so the scent has something to sit on: see our guide to <a href="/blogs/journal/layer-oud-with-body-butter">layering perfume with body butter</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Let it settle</strong> before you judge it. The base notes come through after the first half hour.</li>
</ol>

<ul>

### FAQs

- **What does Tom Ford Black Orchid smell like?** Dark and rich: truffle and plum at the top, rum, ylang-ylang and black orchid in the heart, and patchouli in the base. It is an amber floral, made unisex, and best for evenings.
- **Is Tom Ford Noir Extreme for men?** It is made for men, but anyone can wear it. It is warm and spicy-sweet, with cardamom, nutmeg and saffron, a rose and kulfi heart, and vanilla, amber and sandalwood.
- **Which Tom Ford perfume is best for daytime?** Neroli Portofino: bergamot, mandarin and lemon with neroli, orange flower and lavender, over amber and rosemary. It is fresh, unisex and easy to wear to work.
- **What is the difference between Tom Ford Signature and Private Blend?** They are Tom Ford's two main perfume lines. Black Orchid and Noir Extreme are Signature scents; Oud Wood, Neroli Portofino and Ébène Fumé are Private Blend, usually sold in smaller bottles at a higher price.

### Body

<p>A <strong>face serum</strong> is a light, concentrated step that sits between cleansing and moisturising. Most are watery or gel-like and carry one or two key ingredients, such as vitamin C, hyaluronic acid or niacinamide. Here is what each of those ingredients is, how to choose a serum, and how to layer it.</p>
<h2>Serum, moisturiser or face oil?</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Water-based serums</strong> are thin and quick to absorb. They go on first, after cleansing and toning.</li>
<li><strong>Moisturisers</strong> are creams or lotions that hold moisture in. They go on after your serum.</li>
<li><strong>Oil serums</strong> are blends of plant oils. They can be used last at night, or mixed with a drop of moisturiser.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Vitamin C serum</h2>
<p>Vitamin C is an antioxidant, and one of the most searched serum ingredients. Pure vitamin C (ascorbic acid) can be unstable, so many serums use a gentler, more stable form. <a href="/products/10-vitamin-c-face-serum-20ml-deygas-natural-brightening-even-skin-tone-dark-spots-suitable-for-all-skin-types">Deyga 10% Vitamin C Face Serum</a> (from £16.99, 20ml) uses ascorbyl glucoside, a stable form of vitamin C, with orange and citrus extracts. Use a <strong>vitamin C serum</strong> in the morning, and always finish with sunscreen. See every <a href="/collections/vitamin-c-serum">vitamin C serum</a>.</p>
<h2>Hyaluronic acid serum</h2>
<p>Hyaluronic acid is a moisture-loving ingredient that holds water in the skin, so it suits every skin type, including oily skin. <a href="/products/deyga-2-hyaluronic-acid-serum-20ml">Deyga 2% Hyaluronic Acid Serum</a> (£22.99, 20ml) combines sodium hyaluronate with glycerine, panthenol, aloe vera and centella asiatica, and is made without fragrance. <a href="/products/milk-drops-brightening-serum-40-ml-rice-milk-alpha-arbutin-skin-brightening-treatment-for-dark-spots-uneven-skin-tone">Vilvah Milk Drops Hydrating Serum</a> (from £21.99) pairs aloe vera juice and hyaluronic acid with rice milk extract and alpha arbutin. Apply either to slightly damp skin.</p>
<h2>Niacinamide, bakuchiol and ceramides</h2>
<p>Niacinamide is a form of vitamin B3 that is easy to pair with other ingredients. If you are looking for a <strong>niacinamide serum</strong>, read the full ingredient list: niacinamide often works alongside other ingredients rather than on its own. Bakuchiol is a plant extract often used as a gentle alternative to retinol, and ceramides are lipids that are naturally found in skin. <a href="/products/vilvah-better-ageing-serum-20ml-natural-anti-ageing-firming-wrinkle-reducing-face-serum-with-organic-botanicals">Vilvah Better Ageing Serum</a> (£28.99, 20ml) brings all three together, with hyaluronic acid, olive squalane, blue pea flower extract and a plant peptide extract. The <a href="/products/vilvah-am-pm-serum-kit">Vilvah AM PM Serum Kit</a> (£47.99) pairs it with Milk Drops: Milk Drops in the morning, Better Ageing at night. For dry, mature skin, <a href="/products/moistureburst-hydrating-glow-ceramide-serum">Highborn London MOISTUREBURST Serum</a> (from £57) is a ceramide serum made to layer under moisturiser.</p>
<h2>Face oil serums</h2>
<p>Oil serums suit dry skin and evening routines. <a href="/products/deyga-rose-glow-serum-30ml">Deyga Rose Glow Serum</a> (£17.99, 30ml) blends rose and saffron with coconut, pomegranate seed, rosehip, almond and jojoba oils. <a href="/products/deygas-beautifying-serum-30-ml-natural-glow-even-tone-face-serum-with-organic-oils">Deyga Beautifying Serum</a> (from £17.99, 30ml) uses moringa, kukui, grapeseed and vetiver oils. Warm two or three drops between your palms and press them into the skin.</p>
<h2>How to choose a face serum</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Dry or tight skin:</strong> hyaluronic acid, ceramides or a face oil at night.</li>
<li><strong>Oily skin:</strong> a light hyaluronic acid serum, which can be enough on its own on warm days.</li>
<li><strong>Dull-looking skin:</strong> a vitamin C serum in the morning, with sunscreen.</li>
<li><strong>Sensitive skin:</strong> a short, fragrance-free list, and one new product at a time.</li>
</ul>
<h2>How to layer a serum</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Cleanse</strong> with a face wash that suits you: see <a href="/blogs/journal/face-wash-for-your-skin-type">face wash for your skin type</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Apply 2 or 3 drops</strong> of a water-based serum to slightly damp skin and press it in.</li>
<li><strong>Go thinnest to thickest:</strong> serum, then eye cream, then moisturiser. Read <a href="/blogs/journal/moisturiser-for-your-skin-type">moisturiser for your skin type</a> and our <a href="/blogs/journal/under-eye-cream-guide">under eye cream guide</a>.</li>
<li><strong>In the morning, finish with SPF.</strong> Browse every <a href="/collections/sunscreen">sunscreen</a>.</li>
</ol>
<p>You do not need several serums. One in the morning and one at night is plenty for most people.</p>
<h2>Good to know</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Patch test</strong> a new serum on your jaw or inner arm for 24 hours.</li>
<li><strong>Introduce one new serum at a time,</strong> so you know what suits your skin.</li>
<li><strong>Store serums</strong> with the cap closed, away from heat and sunlight, and use them within the time shown on the pack.</li>
<li><strong>Stinging, redness or itching that lasts</strong> is worth showing to a pharmacist or your GP.</li>
</ul>

### FAQs

- **What does a face serum do?** A serum is a light, concentrated step that delivers one or two key ingredients, such as vitamin C, hyaluronic acid or niacinamide. It goes on after cleansing and before moisturiser.
- **When should I use a vitamin C serum?** In the morning, after cleansing and before moisturiser, and always followed by sunscreen. The Deyga 10% Vitamin C Face Serum uses ascorbyl glucoside, a stable form of vitamin C.
- **Can I use hyaluronic acid and niacinamide together?** Yes, they are easy to pair. The Vilvah Better Ageing Serum contains both, along with bakuchiol and ceramides. Introduce one new product at a time and patch test first.
- **Do I put serum before or after moisturiser?** Before. Go from the thinnest texture to the thickest: serum, then eye cream, then moisturiser, and sunscreen last in the morning. Oil serums can go last at night.
