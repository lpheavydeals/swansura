## How this was done, and its limits
- I could not read a live google.co.uk results page. Google serves a JavaScript page with no results to scripts, DuckDuckGo returned a bot challenge, and Bing returned unrelated pages.
- The "who ranks" lists below therefore come from the web-search tool, whose index is US-based. They show the **UK domains that come up most strongly for each query**. They are not a verified Google UK top 3. Confirm the positions in Semrush (Organic Research → UK) or in an incognito google.co.uk search before acting on them.
- UK monthly volumes (Semrush, Oct 2026) come from this repo's own exports: `seo/semrush_exports/*.xlsx` and `docs/seo/semrush_new_perfumes_results_2026-11-02.csv`. They are the only hard volumes in this report.
- Everything else is labelled either with its source or as "trend signal, no volume".
- The Swansura comparison uses public pages only (fetched 9 Nov 2026).

### Patterns across the queries
1. **Gift queries are won by pages named exactly like the query**: "for her", "for him", "aftershave gift sets", "stocking fillers for her". Generic "Gifts" pages don't rank.
2. **Gift guides and seasonal blogs rank next to category pages**. They carry a year ("2026") and price-band sections.
3. **Arabian/Lattafa results are won by small UK specialists**, not big retailers. Their pages are deep collections with filters, an "Inspired by" angle, an FAQ, a buying guide and "dispatched from the UK".
4. **Price and delivery cues appear in titles and banners**: "from £17.99", "free UK delivery over £25", "under £30".
5. **Indian skincare queries are editorial**. No shop owns them, which is an opening for Swansura.

## 2. Seasonal demand, Nov–Dec (UK)

### Hard facts with sources
- **Q4 is the fragrance season.** Nearly half of annual UK fragrance sales fall in October–December (Circana UK, via [Scento gifting stats](https://www.scento.com/blog/perfume-gifting-statistics-2026); [Circana](https://www.circana.com/post/tis-the-season-for-a-happy-fragrance-holiday)).
- **Gift sets are growing.** In UK prestige beauty, fragrance gift sets were the fastest-growing sets in October 2024, up 20% ([Circana via GCI/Scento](https://www.scento.com/blog/perfume-gifting-statistics-2026)).
- **The Perfume Shop's Christmas 2025** ([Retail Times](https://retailtimes.co.uk/?p=168624), [Retail Week](https://www.retail-week.com/health-and-beauty/the-perfume-shop-reports-sale-increase-driven-by-gifts-and-personalisation/7050330.article)):
  - 1.58m bottles sold between 30 Nov and 24 Dec (+6%), about 63k a day.
  - Gift sets were 16.8% of full-year sales (15.7% in 2024).
  - Online demand for sets rose 62% at the peak.
  - Busiest week was 14–20 Dec; busiest in-store day was 23 Dec.
  - Top sellers: Dior Sauvage, Carolina Herrera Good Girl, Chanel Coco Mademoiselle.
- **Minis and discovery sets** grew 41% Jan–Sep 2025 (US, Circana). This is a trend signal for the UK.
- **Advent calendars.** UK searches for advent calendars were up 103% year on year, with "fragrance advent calendars" at about 3,100 a month ([Scratch, 2022, Beauty Daily by Clarins data](https://scratchmagazine.co.uk/news/beauty-advent-calendars-are-most-searched-for-calendars-in-uk); old data). In 2026 LookFantastic, Boots, John Lewis, Selfridges, The Perfume Shop and Sentier all launched fragrance calendars ([Yorkshire Post](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/recommended/first-look-ps150-lookfantastic-2026-fragrance-advent-calendar-contains-over-ps530-8937462), [Woman&Home](https://www.womanandhome.com/beauty/john-lewis-fragrance-advent-calendar-2026/), [Retail Times](https://retailtimes.co.uk/the-perfume-shop-launches-its-must-have-perfume-advent-calendar-for-spritzmas-2026/)). Most went on sale in Sep–Oct, so it is late for Swansura to launch one in November.
- **General gift sets.** UK Google queries for "gift set" were up 305% in December against the Jan–Nov average ([Picodi 2023](https://www.picodi.com/uk/bargain-hunting/christmas-gifts-2023)).
- **Sweet/gourmand scents.** Searches for "sweet perfumes / vanilla perfume / peach scents" were up 44% year on year ([PerfumeDirect via Heyday, Dec 2025](https://www.heydaymagazine.co.uk/2025/12/03/revealed-the-uks-most-wanted-fragrances-for-christmas-2025-from-surprise-budget-heroes-to-the-perfumes-topping-every-wishlist/)). The same article names budget Christmas "failsafes": Jimmy Choo EDP, D&G Light Blue, CK Eternity Moment, Burberry Weekend, Marc Jacobs Daisy.
- **Dupes.** Interest in dupes is rising ([Mintel UK Fragrances 2025](https://store.mintel.com/report/uk-fragrances-market-report-2025), numbers paywalled). The global Google index for "dupe perfume" rose 8.9× between Apr 2020 and Mar 2025 (Playbook of Beauty via [Scento](https://www.scento.com/blog/uk-fragrance-statistics-2026)). I found no UK volume for "smells like …" queries.
- **Arabian brands.** Lattafa sold more than US$63m on TikTok Shop in a year ([SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/magazines/style/beauty/trends/article/3360073/middle-eastern-perfumes-are-trending-led-lattafa-and-mona-kattans-kayali)) and was the top fragrance brand on TikTok Shop ([newsminimalist](https://www.newsminimalist.com/articles/lattafa-becomes-top-fragrance-brand-on-tiktok-shop-e022961f)). These are global, not UK, figures.

## 3. Who is searching (Nov/Dec)

- **Gift buyers for a partner or family.**
  - 28% of UK perfume users rarely buy perfume for themselves; they mainly give or receive it as a gift. Men are slightly more gift-involved (30% vs 27%).
  - Scent drives 86% of purchases, price/value 50% and brand 21% (men 28%).
  - 39% buy from online retailers.
  - Discovery is mainly in-store sampling (63%) and friends and family (30%); social media is 9%.
  - Source: [YouGov GB, 14 Oct 2025, n=800](https://yougov.com/en-gb/articles/53212-scent-first-brand-later-how-britons-buy-their-perfume).
  - Implication: gift buyers often don't know the scent, so they buy safe bestsellers and sets. The page has to explain the scent in plain words.
- **Last-minute buyers.** Christmas fragrance spend concentrates in the last 10 days, with The Perfume Shop's peak on 14–20 Dec and 23 Dec. Delivery cut-off messaging matters from about 10 Dec ([Retail Times](https://retailtimes.co.uk/?p=168624)).
- **Value seekers and students.**
  - Save the Student and deal sites rank for "cheap designer perfume".
  - Retailers report younger shoppers driving fragrance growth ([Retail Gazette, Feb 2026](https://www.retailgazette.co.uk/blog/2026/02/why-perfume-is-thriving/), qualitative only).
  - An unverified third-party figure puts 14% of The Fragrance Shop's online customers at 18–24.
  - The Gen Z "scent wardrobe" and TikTok dupe culture is driven by Lattafa, Armaf, Maison Alhambra and Al Haramain ([Scento UK stats](https://www.scento.com/blog/uk-fragrance-statistics-2026); [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/magazines/style/beauty/trends/article/3360073/middle-eastern-perfumes-are-trending-led-lattafa-and-mona-kattans-kayali)). This audience searches brand plus product name (Khamrah, Yara, Club de Nuit), watches TikTok, and is price-sensitive (£20–£45).
- **Arabian/oud enthusiasts, Muslim and Middle Eastern UK communities.** Specialist UK shops (Birmingham, Leicester, London) serve this group with attars, oils and "authorised seller" trust lines (Opulensi, Emirates Oud, Perfume Closet). No demographic data found.
- **South Asian diaspora and Ayurveda-curious buyers for Indian skincare.**
  - UK "Ayurveda beauty" searches were up 213% in 12 months, and LookFantastic reported "Ayurveda" searches up 71.67% (trade report, 2024, via [Cosmetics Business](https://cosmeticsbusiness.com/south-asian-beauty-is-booming-in-western-markets)).
  - Kama Ayurveda (2023) and Kay Beauty (2025) launched in the UK.
  - Middle-income buyers use Himalaya/Biotique-type brands; luxury buyers use Forest Essentials/Kama.
  - A 2022 report says the UK beauty industry misses £2.7bn a year by not serving multi-ethnic customers ([Professional Beauty](https://professionalbeauty.co.uk/site/newsdetails/beauty-brands-miss-out-on-2-7-billion-per-year-by)).
  - No UK-diaspora spend figure was found. Diwali (8 Nov) has passed, so the next angle is Christmas gifting to family.
- **Natural/"clean" gift buyers** (mostly women gifting women, mum or friend). NOTHS dominates. Their cues are "organic/vegan", "made in UK" and award badges. Swansura's honest equivalents are "plant-based Indian brand", "official UK partner" and the ingredient list.

## 4. What top pages do that a small store can copy, and quick wins

### Competitor page features worth copying
- **One category page per gift intent**, named exactly like the query (LF, Superdrug, Next, Notino, M&S). Examples: "Perfume gift sets for her", "Aftershave gift sets for him", "Stocking fillers for her".
- **Filters by recipient, price band, scent family and type**, with "Best seller", "Sale" and struck-through-price badges (Soghaat, LF).
- **A collapsible buying guide plus an FAQ of 10–15 questions** on the category page (Soghaat Lattafa: where to buy, longevity, which for men or women, gifting, delivery).
- **"Explore related collections" pills** (Soghaat has 7; Swansura already has these).
- **A dated gift guide** ("Best perfume gift sets UK 2026") with sections by recipient and budget, linking to products (P-Eleven, Cult Beauty, Perfume Shop).
- **A permanent Black Friday URL** (Google advises one evergreen /black-friday URL reused every year rather than dated URLs: [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-recommends-permanent-urls-for-black-friday-pages/386384/), [MediaPost/Mueller](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/328160/googles-john-mueller-one-seasonal-url-would-coll.html)).
- **Trust strip**: free-delivery threshold, "dispatched from London/UK", "authorised seller / 100% authentic", Trustpilot rating, gift wrap (P-Eleven, Perfume Shop).
- **Structured data**: Product + Offer with shipping and returns ([Google return-policy docs](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/return-policy)), plus Merchant Center free listings for the Shopping tab. Swansura already has the product JSON-LD.

   - Buying peaks 14–23 Dec, and the store already has Standard, Two-day and Next-day rates.
