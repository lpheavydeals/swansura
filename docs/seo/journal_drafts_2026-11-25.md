## Guerlain perfume: Habit Rouge, Samsara, L'Instant, La Petite Robe Noire and more compared

Guerlain perfume runs from classic bottles such as Habit Rouge and Samsara to the modern Mon Guerlain and the richer, unisex fragrances of its niche line. Here is every Guerlain fragrance we stock, what is in each one, and who it suits.

### Guerlain Habit Rouge

Guerlain Habit Rouge Eau de Parfum (100ml) is the men's classic of the house. Four citrus notes, lemon, bergamot, orange and lime, open it; rose, jasmine, cinnamon and patchouli warm the heart; and leather, vanilla, amber, benzoin and labdanum make a smooth base. Bright at first, then warm and polished, it suits the office as well as evenings.

### Guerlain Samsara

Guerlain Samsara Eau de Parfum (75ml) is a warm floral built on sandalwood. Bergamot, peach and ylang-ylang lead into jasmine, rose, narcissus and orris, and the base is sandalwood, vanilla, amber, tonka bean and musk. Smooth rather than sharp, it is a good choice for someone who likes rich, creamy florals.

### L'Instant de Guerlain

L'Instant de Guerlain Eau de Parfum (75ml) is a honeyed floral. Mandarin orange, bergamot and red apple open it; magnolia, jasmine, ylang-ylang and iris form the heart; and honey, vanilla, amber, benzoin and musk settle on the skin. It sits between Samsara's depth and the lightness of La Petite Robe Noire.

### La Petite Robe Noire Ma Robe Cocktail

La Petite Robe Noire Ma Robe Cocktail Eau de Toilette (100ml) is the lightest Guerlain we stock: bergamot, lemon, orange and red berries, then rose, jasmine and cherry, over white amber, musk and patchouli. Fruity, playful and easy to wear every day.

### The Mon Guerlain miniature set

The Mon Guerlain Miniature Perfume Gift Set holds four 5ml bottles, 20ml in all:

Mon Guerlain Eau de Parfum (2 x 5ml): lavender, bergamot, jasmine sambac, iris, Tahitian vanilla and sandalwood.

Mon Guerlain Florale Eau de Parfum (5ml): lavender, jasmine sambac, peony, neroli, vanilla and sandalwood.

Mon Guerlain Bloom of Rose Eau de Toilette (5ml): Bulgarian and Turkish rose, lavender, neroli, vanilla and sandalwood.

Lavender and vanilla run through all three, so it is an easy way to find the Mon Guerlain that suits you, and a ready-made gift.

### The richer unisex scents: Oud Essentiel and Épices Exquises

Guerlain Oud Essentiel Eau de Parfum (125ml) frames oud with saffron and pelargonium at the top, rose and cedarwood in the heart, and leather, frankincense and guaiac wood in the base. For more oud scents, see our oud perfume guide.

Guerlain Épices Exquises Eau de Parfum (125ml) is built on spice and coffee: cardamom, pink pepper and angelica, then black pepper and coffee, over sandalwood, oud and patchouli. Guerlain Musc Noble, from the same line, is sold out at the moment.

To try several, or as a gift: the Mon Guerlain miniature set.

### Another warm scent in a similar mood

If you like the amber and vanilla in Habit Rouge or Samsara, try Chopard Amber Malaki Eau de Parfum (80ml): orange blossom and frankincense, then amber and papyrus, over labdanum and bourbon vanilla. It is a different perfume, not a copy.

Carolina Herrera perfume for men comes in two families we stock: Bad Boy, in its lightning-bolt bottle, and 212, the house's city-inspired line. Here is every Carolina Herrera fragrance we have, what is in each one, and how to choose between them.

### Bad Boy Le Parfum

Bad Boy Le Parfum is an aromatic, leathery eau de parfum for men, in a 150ml bottle or a 10ml size for travel. Grapefruit and hemp open it, black pepper and geranium add spice and a green warmth, and leather with vetiver gives a dark, dry base. It starts sharp and citrusy, then turns smoky as the pepper and leather come through.

### Bad Boy Extreme

Bad Boy Extreme Eau de Parfum, in stock in the 10ml size, is the sweeter, warmer version: ginger and bergamot, then cacao and vetiver, over patchouli and tonka bean. The cacao and tonka make it dark and sweet-spiced, so it suits evenings and nights out.

Le Parfum or Extreme? Choose Le Parfum for a dry, leathery scent with pepper; choose Extreme if you like cocoa and tonka sweetness.

### 212 VIP Men

212 VIP Men Eau de Toilette (100ml) is the bright, playful one. Lime, passion fruit, pepper and ginger open it, a cocktail heart of vodka, gin, mint and spices follows, and amber, leather and woody notes give it a warm base. Fresh and lively, it is made for parties and weekends.

### 212 Sexy Men

212 Sexy Men Eau de Toilette (100ml) is warmer and softer: mandarin orange, bergamot and green notes, then cardamom, pepper and flowers, over vanilla, sandalwood, guaiac wood, musk and amber. A smooth, spiced scent for evenings and cooler months.

### Which Carolina Herrera perfume should you choose?

Dry, smoky and leathery: Bad Boy Le Parfum.

Sweet, with cocoa and tonka: Bad Boy Extreme.

Fresh and fun: 212 VIP Men.

Soft, warm and spiced: 212 Sexy Men.

To try before a big bottle: the 10ml Bad Boy Le Parfum or Bad Boy Extreme.

These are different perfumes, not copies:

If you like 212 Sexy Men's warm spice: Joop! Homme Le Parfum (75ml), with lavender and cinnamon, orange blossom and tonka bean, over vanilla, amber and woods.
