Mugler's fragrances fall into a few clear families: Alien, built around jasmine and amber; Angel, sweet and gourmand; the fresh Mugler Colognes; and the Les Exceptions collection. Here is every **Mugler perfume** we stock, with its notes, the sizes on offer and who each one suits.

It is a statement scent for evenings and colder weather.

Cold wind, central heating and hot drinks all leave lips dry in winter, and a good **lip balm** is the simplest fix. But balms are not all the same: some carry an SPF, some add a sheer tint, and some are just a few plant oils, butters and wax. Here is how to choose a **lip balm for dry lips**, what is inside each type and how to get the most from it.

## What a lip balm does

Lips have a very thin outer layer and no oil glands of their own, so they dry out faster than the rest of the face. A balm puts a soft layer of oils, butters and wax over them, which keeps them feeling comfortable for longer. Most balms are built from the same few things: plant oils such as almond, avocado or jojoba; butters such as shea, mango or kokum; and a wax, often beeswax, that holds it all together.

## How to use a lip balm

- **Start with clean, dry lips.**

- **Apply a thin layer** with the stick or a clean fingertip, and reapply whenever lips feel dry.

- **At night, use a thicker layer** so it can sit on your lips while you sleep.

- **Once or twice a week, use a lip scrub first.** A balm spreads more evenly on smooth lips; our [lip scrub guide](/blogs/journal/how-to-use-lip-scrub) explains how.

- **In the sun, choose an SPF balm** and reapply often.

## Good to know

- **Try not to lick your lips:** saliva dries quickly and leaves them drier than before.

- **Many balms contain beeswax.** If you avoid it, check the Ingredients tab on each product before you buy.

- **Patch test** balms with essential oils, such as the lemon and grapefruit balms, before first use.

- **Cracked or sore lips** that do not settle after a week or two are worth showing to a pharmacist or your GP.

## FAQs

**What is the best lip balm for dry lips?**  
One with plant butters and oils that sits on the lips for a while, such as shea butter, almond oil and beeswax. Apply a thin layer through the day and a thicker one at night, and use a gentle lip scrub once or twice a week so the balm spreads evenly.

**Do I need an SPF lip balm in winter?**  
Lips can burn on bright winter days, on ski trips and on holiday, so an SPF balm is worth carrying. The Vilvah Milk Lip Balm SPF 30 PA+++ uses zinc oxide and titanium dioxide. Reapply often, especially after eating or drinking.

**What is the difference between a tinted lip balm and a lipstick?**  
A tinted lip balm is a moisturising balm with a sheer wash of colour, so it feels like a balm and gives a soft, natural look. A lipstick gives stronger colour. Our tinted balms take their colour from beetroot extract, fruit extracts or mineral pigments.

**How often should I apply lip balm?**  
Whenever your lips feel dry, and always before bed. In cold or windy weather you may need it several times a day. If you are outdoors in the sun, use an SPF lip balm and reapply often.
