## What is a lip scrub?

A lip scrub is a small pot of fine sugar held in butters and oils. The sugar does the gentle polishing; the butters and oils leave a soft, cushioned layer behind. Ours are short, simple blends:

## How to use a lip scrub

- **Start with clean, dry lips.** Take off lipstick or balm first.
- **Take a small amount,** about the size of a grain of rice, on a clean fingertip.
- **Massage in small circles** for 30 seconds to a minute. Use light pressure: let the sugar do the work.
- **Wipe or rinse it off** with a soft, damp cloth or lukewarm water. Some people simply lick the sugar away.
- **Apply a lip balm straight away,** while lips are soft and smooth.

## How often should you use a lip scrub?

For most people, **two or three times a week** is plenty. Lip skin is thin, so scrubbing every day can leave it feeling sore. In winter, or if your lips are very dry, use it when they feel flaky rather than on a fixed schedule. Before a matte or long-wear lipstick, a quick scrub helps the colour go on more evenly.

## Good to know

- **Patch test first,** especially with scrubs and balms that contain essential oils such as mint, lemon or grapefruit.
- **Skip the scrub** on cracked, cut, sore or sunburnt lips, or during a cold sore. Use only a balm until they have settled.
- **Lips that stay sore,** split or swollen for a long time are worth showing to a pharmacist or your GP.
- **Keep pots clean:** use a clean fingertip and close the lid after use.

**FAQs:**

- **How often should I use a lip scrub?** Two or three times a week suits most people. Lip skin is thin, so daily scrubbing can leave it sore. Use it when your lips feel flaky, and always follow with a lip balm.
- **Should I use lip balm after a lip scrub?** Yes. A scrub lifts dry, flaky skin; a balm straight afterwards keeps lips soft. Choose a natural balm for everyday, a tinted balm for colour, or an SPF lip balm for daytime outdoors.
- **Can I use a lip scrub before lipstick?** Yes. A gentle scrub a few minutes before lipstick helps the colour go on more evenly, especially matte or long-wear shades. Apply a thin layer of balm first and blot it before the lipstick.
- **When should I not use a lip scrub?** Skip it on cracked, cut, sore or sunburnt lips and during a cold sore. Use only a balm until they have settled, and speak to a pharmacist if they stay sore.

## EDT, EDP and Parfum: what the labels mean

The label tells you how much perfume oil is in the bottle. The ranges below are typical; every house sets its own.

- **Eau de toilette (EDT):** typically around 5–15% perfume oil. Lighter, with a bright opening that suits daytime and warm weather.
- **Eau de parfum (EDP):** usually around 15–20%. Fuller and rounder, and the most popular strength today.
- **Parfum (also called extrait):** the most concentrated form, typically around 20–40%. Richer and closer to the skin, so a little goes further.

A higher concentration does not simply mean "stronger". Perfumers often rework the formula for each strength, so the notes, the balance and the feel on skin change too. We explain this in more detail in [extrait vs eau de parfum: what the label means](/blogs/journal/extrait-vs-eau-de-parfum).

## Dior Sauvage eau de parfum: the notes

- **Top:** Calabrian bergamot and Sichuan pepper.
- **Heart:** star anise, nutmeg and lavender.

Compared with an eau de toilette, an EDP like this tends to feel softer and warmer, with the vanilla and amber showing more as it settles. It works year-round, and especially in the evening and in cooler months.

Here is what is in each one, how the strengths compare, and how to choose, whether for yourself or as a gift.

## Calvin Klein Eternity for women

[Calvin Klein Eternity Eau de Parfum](/products/calvin-klein-eternity-eau-de-parfum-women-s-classic-floral-fragrance) (from £28.99, 30ml or 100ml) is a classic floral: fresh citrus at the start, a big bouquet of white and spring flowers in the middle, and a soft woody base.

- **Top:** freesia, mandarin orange, sage and citrus.
- **Heart:** lily, carnation, violet, jasmine, rose, lily of the valley, marigold and narcissus.
- **Base:** sandalwood, amber, musk, heliotrope and patchouli.

It is graceful, clean and easy to wear: a good everyday scent for the office, weddings and special days alike.

## Calvin Klein Eternity for men

[Calvin Klein Eternity for Men Eau de Toilette](/products/calvin-klein-eternity-for-men-eau-de-toilette-100ml-men-s-fresh-aromatic-fragrance) (£38.99, 100ml) is a fresh aromatic fragrance: citrus and lavender first, then herbs, then warm woods.

- **Top:** mandarin orange, lavender, bergamot and lemon.
- **Heart:** sage, juniper berries, basil, geranium, jasmine and coriander.
- **Base:** sandalwood, vetiver, musk, amber and Brazilian rosewood.

It feels crisp and clean, with a warm finish, and suits daytime and work as well as evenings.

## Eternity for women vs for men: the key differences

- **Character:** the women's Eternity is floral, built around lily, carnation and rose. The men's is aromatic, built around lavender and herbs such as sage, juniper and basil.
- **What they share:** mandarin and sage near the top, and sandalwood, amber and musk in the base. That is why they feel like a pair.
- **Strength:** we stock the women's Eternity as an eau de parfum and the men's as an eau de toilette. An eau de parfum typically holds around 15–20% perfume oil and an eau de toilette around 5–15%, so the women's feels a little fuller on skin. Read [what the labels mean](/blogs/journal/extrait-vs-eau-de-parfum).
- **Who can wear them:** anyone. Many women enjoy the men's lavender freshness, and many men like the women's soft florals. Choose by the notes you love.
