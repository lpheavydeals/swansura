### Versace Eros: mint, apple and vanilla
Versace Eros Eau de Toilette (£68.99, 200ml) is a men's fragrance built on contrast. Mint, Italian lemon, mandarin and candied apple open it; geranium, clary sage and an amber note sit in the heart; and two cedarwoods, vetiver, patchouli, sandalwood and vanilla make the base. #### Versace Eros Parfum
Versace Eros Parfum (from £68.99, 100ml or 200ml) is the darker, more resinous version. Where the eau de toilette leans on mint and apple, the parfum leans on pepper, lavender and resin.


Eros eau de toilette or Eros Parfum? Choose the eau de toilette if you like the minty, sweet apple opening; choose the parfum for a spicier, deeper scent. ## Peptide serum and bakuchiol serum: what they are and how to use them
/blogs/journal/peptide-bakuchiol-serum-guide · SEO title: Peptide Serum and Bakuchiol Serum Explained | Swansura · description: What peptides, bakuchiol and ceramides are, how to use a peptide or bakuchiol serum, and the serums we stock from Highborn London and Vilvah.

Peptides and bakuchiol are two of the most searched serum ingredients in the UK, often alongside ceramides. They do different jobs and suit different routines. Here is what each one is, how to use a peptide serum or a bakuchiol serum, and the serums we stock that contain them.



### What are peptides?
Peptides are short chains of amino acids, the building blocks of proteins. In skincare they are made in the lab or taken from plants, and they usually appear in serums and creams in small amounts. Different peptides have different trade names, such as Matrixyl or Argireline, so a peptide serum often lists several of them together.

### What is bakuchiol?
Bakuchiol is a plant-derived ingredient, taken from the seeds of the babchi plant (Psoralea corylifolia). It is often chosen instead of retinol by people who prefer a plant-based ingredient. It can usually be used morning and night; as with any new serum, patch test first.

It brings peptides, bakuchiol and ceramides together in one bottle, in a light texture.



### What are ceramides?
Ceramides are lipids that occur naturally in the outer layer of skin. In skincare they are added to help skin feel smooth and comfortable, and they pair well with peptides or bakuchiol. Highborn London MOISTUREBURST Hydrating Glow Ceramide Serum (from £57, 30ml or 50ml) is a lightweight ceramide serum for dry, dehydrated and mature skin, made to layer under moisturiser.
