Frizz is one of the most common hair complaints, and it is usually a sign that hair is dry. The good news is that the fix is mostly moisture and a gentle routine, not a cupboard full of products. Here is what causes frizz, when to reach for a **hair serum for frizzy hair**, a pre-wash oil or a **hair mask for dry hair**, and how to put them together.

## Why hair goes frizzy

Each strand has a smooth outer layer. When hair is dry, that layer lifts a little, strands lose their shine and they take in moisture from damp air, so they swell and stop lying flat. Rough towel drying, hot water, heat styling and brushing hair while it is wet all make it worse. That is why frizz often gets worse in winter, when indoor heating dries hair out, and on wet British days.

## Serum, oil or mask: what each one does


- **A hair serum** is a light leave-in for the lengths and ends. It smooths the surface of dry hair after washing, so flyaways lie flatter and hair looks glossier.
- **A hair oil** is usually richer and used before washing, on the scalp and lengths, then shampooed out.
- **A hair mask** is a thicker conditioner you leave on for longer, once or twice a week, for hair that feels rough and dry.

Most dry, frizzy hair does well with all three in their place: a mask weekly, a conditioner every wash and a few drops of serum to finish.

## A simple routine for frizzy hair


- **Once a week, before washing:** massage a little oil into the scalp and lengths and leave it on for an hour.
- **Wash with lukewarm water,** not hot, and a creamy shampoo. Lather mainly at the scalp.
- **Condition every time,** from the mid-lengths to the ends. Once or twice a week, swap the conditioner for a hair mask.
- **Blot, do not rub.** Squeeze water out with a soft towel or an old cotton T-shirt.
- **Detangle gently** with a wide-tooth comb, starting at the ends. ## Good to know


- **Less is more with serum and oil:** start with a few drops and add more only if your ends still feel dry.
- **Patch test** products with essential oils, such as the serum and the pre-wash oil, before first use.
- **Silk or satin pillowcases** and a loose plait at night can help hair stay smoother by morning.
- **Sudden changes** in your hair or scalp, such as itching, soreness or hair loss, are worth showing to a pharmacist or your GP.

## FAQs

- **What is the best hair serum for frizzy hair?** A light serum you can smooth over the ends without weighing hair down. - **Is a hair mask or a hair oil better for dry hair?** They do different jobs. A hair oil is usually used before washing and shampooed out; a hair mask is a rich conditioner you leave on for 10 to 15 minutes after shampooing. Many people with dry hair use an oil once a week and a mask once or twice a week.
- **How often should I use a hair mask?** Once or twice a week is plenty for most dry or frizzy hair. On the other wash days, use a regular conditioner from the mid-lengths to the ends.
