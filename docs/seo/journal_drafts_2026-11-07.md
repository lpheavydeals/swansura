A good cleanse is the first step of every skincare routine, and the wrong **face wash** can undo the rest. Too harsh, and skin feels tight and dry; too rich, and oily skin feels heavy. Here is how to choose a face wash for your skin type, what the different textures and ingredients mean, and how to cleanse.

## First, know your skin type

Wash with a mild cleanser, pat dry and wait about an hour without applying anything. Then look and feel:

- **Dry:** feels tight, and may look flaky or dull.
- **Oily:** looks shiny all over, especially on the forehead, nose and chin.
- **Combination:** shiny in the centre, normal or dry on the cheeks.
- **Sensitive:** easily stings, turns red or reacts to fragrance.

## How to wash your face

- **Wet your face** with lukewarm water, not hot.
- **Use a small amount,** about the size of a 5p coin, and work it into a lather in your hands.
- **Massage gently** for 30 to 60 seconds, including the hairline and jaw. Keep it away from your eyes.
- **Rinse well** and pat dry with a clean towel.
- **Follow straight away** with toner or moisturiser while skin is still slightly damp.

Most people do best washing twice a day: morning and night. If you wear make-up or sunscreen, cleanse twice at night: once to lift it, once to clean the skin.

## How to wash your hair

- **Wet your hair thoroughly** with warm, not hot, water.
- **Use a coin-sized amount** of shampoo, more for long or thick hair.
- **Focus on the scalp,** massaging with your fingertips, not your nails. The lather will clean the lengths as you rinse.
- **Rinse well,** then apply conditioner to the mid-lengths and ends.
- **Leave it for a minute or two,** comb through with a wide-tooth comb, and rinse.
- **Pat dry** with a towel rather than rubbing, which can rough up the hair.

How often? Dry or curly hair often does well with two or three washes a week; an oily scalp may prefer every other day. Natural shampoos can lather less than you are used to, so a second, smaller wash is fine.
