### Body

<p>A <strong>face toner</strong> is the light, watery step between cleansing and moisturising. Old-fashioned toners were often astringent and full of alcohol, which is why many people with dry skin still avoid them. Today most toners are gentle and hydrating instead. Here is how to choose a <strong>toner for dry skin</strong>, which toner suits oily or sensitive skin, and how to use one.</p>
<h2>What does a toner do?</h2>
<p>After cleansing, a toner takes off any last traces of cleanser or tap water and gives skin a first layer of moisture. Skin that is slightly damp takes serum and moisturiser more evenly, so a toner makes the rest of your routine easier to apply. It is an optional step: if your skin feels comfortable without one, you can skip it.</p>
<h2>How to choose a toner for dry skin</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Look for hydrating ingredients</strong> such as glycerine, rose water or aloe near the top of the list.</li>
<li><strong>Avoid a high level of alcohol</strong> (listed as alcohol denat. or SD alcohol), which can leave dry skin feeling tight.</li>
<li><strong>Keep it short</strong> if your skin is sensitive too: fewer ingredients make it easier to see what suits you.</li>
<li><strong>Go easy on acids</strong> such as glycolic acid, which gently exfoliate. Dry skin may prefer them two or three times a week rather than daily.</li>
</ul>

</ul>
<h2>How to use a toner</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Cleanse</strong> with a gentle face wash such as <a href="/products/deyga-oat-milk-face-wash-100-ml">Deyga Oat Milk Face Wash</a> (£12.99), then pat your face almost dry. <li><strong>Apply the toner</strong> with clean hands or a cotton pad, pressing it over the face and neck. Avoid the eyes.</li>
<li><strong>While skin is still damp,</strong> apply a serum such as <a href="/products/deyga-2-hyaluronic-acid-serum-20ml">Deyga 2% Hyaluronic Acid Serum</a> (£22.99). Read our <a href="/blogs/journal/face-serum-guide">face serum guide</a>.</li>

<li><strong>In the morning, add sunscreen</strong> as the last step.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Good to know</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Toner is not a cleanser:</strong> it will not take off make-up or sunscreen on its own.</li>
<li><strong>Rose water also mixes face packs:</strong> see our <a href="/blogs/journal/face-mask-guide">face mask guide</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Patch test</strong> any new toner on your jaw or inner arm for 24 hours.</li>
<li><strong>Stinging, redness or flaking that lasts</strong> is worth showing to a pharmacist or your GP.</li>
</ul>

### FAQs

- **What is the best toner for dry skin?** A hydrating toner without a high level of alcohol, such as the Deyga Pure Rose Water Toner (rose and distilled water only) or the Highborn London Diamond Toner. Apply it after cleansing, then serum and moisturiser while skin is still damp.
- **Do I need a toner if I have dry skin?** It is optional. A hydrating toner gives skin a first layer of moisture and helps serum and moisturiser spread evenly, but if your skin feels comfortable without one, you can skip it.
- **Which toner is good for oily skin?** A light toner without alcohol, such as the Deyga Tea Tree Facial Toner, made with tea tree, mulethi and distilled water. Patch test it first if your skin is sensitive.
- **Can I use a toner with glycolic acid on dry skin?** Yes, but gently. Start with two or three evenings a week, follow with moisturiser, and wear sunscreen during the day. The Vilvah Milk Face Toner contains glycolic acid.
