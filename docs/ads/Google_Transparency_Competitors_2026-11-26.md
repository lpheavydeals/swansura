## What it means for Swansura
1. Designer best-sellers (Dior, Gucci, Tom Ford, Versace, Paco Rabanne) are where The Perfume Shop, The Fragrance Shop, Boots, Notino and US discounters spend most. They show star ratings and big Black Friday discounts. A new account at £10/day will lose most of those auctions unless our price is clearly lower.
2. Niche and Arabian lines (Al Haramain, Tiziana Terenzi, Jo Milano / Game of Spades, Thomas Kosmala, Maison Alhambra, Lattafa when in stock) have few UK advertisers: Al Haramain itself 6 live ads, Arabian Oud 15, Lattafa none. Start Shopping there.
3. Our delivery offer matches the leaders (free over £40, next-day £3.99); our returns (14 days, unopened) are shorter than TFS's 90+ days. We have no seller rating stars (Google needs about 100 shop reviews in 12 months).
4. Vilvah runs only 1 UK Google ad and Deyga none: brand searches 'vilvah' / 'deyga' are almost uncontested and cheap.
5. Black Friday week (to 1 Dec) is the most expensive time to start: launch 1 Dec.
