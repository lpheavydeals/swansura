About this page

Skincare routine sets for skin, hair, body and scent

A good skincare routine does not need ten steps. Our routines put two or three products in the right order, so you can shop a complete regime in one go. There are 16 in total, for skin, hair, body and fragrance, built mostly from Deyga and Vilvah, the Indian brands Swansura is an official UK partner of, plus Highborn London, anatomē and Al Haramain. Everything ships from the UK.

Skin

The Clear Skin Routine pairs Deyga Charcoal Face Wash, Tea Tree Facial Toner and Neem Seed Oil for oily skin. The Vitamin C Glow Routine runs from foaming wash to 10% Vitamin C serum to Deyga Sunscreen. For skincare for dry skin, the Deep Hydration Routine combines Vilvah Honey Face Wash, Deyga 2% Hyaluronic Acid Serum and Deep Moisturising Face Cream. The Milk Glow Routine uses Vilvah's milk range, the Sensitive Skin Routine starts with a fragrance-free cleanser and pure aloe, and the Age-Defying Routine brings together Highborn London Crystal Cleanser, YOUTHBOOST serum and Imperial Moisturiser.

Hair and body

The Hair Fall Rescue oils the roots, washes gently and conditions the lengths with Vilvah. The Smooth & Shine Routine pairs Deyga Rice Water Shampoo and Soy Milk Conditioner with Vilvah Anti-Frizz Hair Serum, and makes a thoughtful hair care gift set. The Calm Scalp Duo is an oil and shampoo for flakes. For the body, the Body Polish Ritual adds a body scrub between wash and butter, and the Foot Spa at Home ends with Vilvah Foot Care Cream, a rich foot cream with shea and mango butter. The Lip Wardrobe starts with a lip scrub and finishes with SPF, and the Sleep Ritual uses anatomē bath salts, sleep oil and pillow spray.

Scent

Fragrance lasts longer on moisturised skin, and every skincare routine can end with scent, so the Amber Oud Evening pairs Deyga Chocolate Body Butter with Al Haramain Amber Oud Ruby Edition. The Amber Oud Discovery lets you compare Bleu, White and Gold editions, and the Gentleman's Set brings a charcoal body wash, a neem wood comb and Fumeri Blue For Men together.
