Good to know

Routine questions

What is a routine?

A set of 2 to 3 products chosen to be used together, with each step listed in order. There are 16 routines across skincare, hair, body and skin + scent.

How much do I save on a routine?

Buying the full routine saves 5% compared with buying each product on its own.

Can I buy one product from a routine?

Yes. Every product in a routine links to its own page, where you can buy it on its own.

In what order should I use the products?

Follow the numbered steps on each routine card. Skincare usually goes cleanse, tone or treat, then moisturise, with sunscreen last in the morning.

Do you also sell ready-made kits?

Yes. There are 116 brand kits, miniature sets and gift sets in Gifts & Sets.
