About Swansura

Swansura – The Body Studio is a UK online store for natural skincare and fine fragrance. We are an official UK partner of Deyga and Vilvah, and we stock niche, Arabian and designer perfume houses including Tiziana Terenzi, Al Haramain, Hemel and Gucci. Every product is 100% authentic and shipped from the UK.
