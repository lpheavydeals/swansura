Good to know

Advent calendar questions

What is the difference between the two calendars?

The Skin Studio Advent (£59) has 12 natural skin, lip and body products from Deyga and Vilvah. The Scent Studio Advent (£119) has 12 designer and niche fragrance miniatures.

How much are the contents worth?

Skin Studio contents are worth £110.88 and Scent Studio contents £213.80, based on Swansura's individual prices.

Are the products full size?

The Skin Studio Advent is mostly full-size lip, soap and skincare favourites. The Scent Studio Advent is fragrance miniatures between 5ml and 20ml.

Is it a good gift?

Yes. Each calendar arrives gift-ready, and it is a good way to try a brand or fragrance before buying the full size.

When should I buy?

Order by late November so it arrives before 1 December. Calendars are limited and sell out quickly.
