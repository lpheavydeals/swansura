Ads start only after the website and basic social media are fixed, so paid traffic lands on a store and profile that can convert. Days 1–30 are the gate: reviews visible, dead buttons fixed, catalogue cleaned, sitemaps submitted, brand pages live, Instagram reset with fresh content.
