Recommendation · Brand pages

Brand names bring the traffic

Shoppers search by brand. Today Deyga has no brand page (/collections/deyga is a 404), Vilvah sits under a product name, and the homepage "Shop Brand" buttons lead nowhere.

Every brand page has

  • H1: "Deyga UK – Official Stockist"
  • 150–200 words of story and FAQ
  • Bestsellers first, then the range

Linked from

  • Homepage "Shop by brand" logo strip
  • Brands A–Z page in the main menu
  • Every product page and blog post

Build first: Deyga, Vilvah, Tiziana Terenzi, Al Haramain, Montale, Mancera.

Sources: Search Console, PageSpeed, Meta Ad Library, swansura.com & Claude

Swansura Growth Audit · 18

Homepage links pass the most authority, so brand pages linked from the homepage rank faster. Swansura already ranks #3 for "Deyga UK" with no brand page at all, so a proper Deyga page linked from the homepage is the quickest ranking win. Some fragrance brands already have collections (al-haramain, tiziana-terenzi, mancera, montale); they need copy, clean titles and homepage links. Designer fragrance brands follow after the first six.
