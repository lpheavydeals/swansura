Swansura Growth Audit · 19

Modelled on Indicrest UK, a UK Indian-beauty retailer with a strong SEO structure: a 3-level menu (Skincare › Cleansers › Face Cleanser), flat keyword URLs (/collections/serums, /collections/forest-essentials), a Brands A–Z index with about 200 brand pages, concern pages such as Hairfall & Thinning and Dandruff, and Shop by Fragrance Family (Oud, Floral, Woody). Brand page URLs like /collections/deyga. What we would NOT copy: the same sub-collection under several parents (duplicate content) and A–Z letter links that Google cannot crawl. This replaces today's 15 empty and duplicate collections and the oddly named Vilvah collection. Every category and brand page gets an H1, intro copy, FAQ and breadcrumbs.
