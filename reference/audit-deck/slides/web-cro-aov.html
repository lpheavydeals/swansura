Conversion (CRO)

  • Star rating and review count under the title
  • Trust row: official partner, authentic, returns
  • Delivery time and free UK delivery over £50
  • Sticky Add to Cart; Apple Pay and Shop Pay
  • Tabs: ingredients, how to use, FAQ
  • Back-in-stock alert, not a dead "Sold out"

Average order value (AOV)

  • "Complete the ritual" bundle with a saving
  • "Frequently bought together" add-ons
  • Cart bar: "£12 away from free delivery"
  • Under-£10 add-ons: lip balm, perfume mini
  • Spend £60, get a free mini
  • Gift box option on fragrance and sets

Goal: average order value of £35–50, where Meta Ads pay back on the first order.

Sources: Search Console, PageSpeed, Meta Ad Library, swansura.com & Claude
